# World Geography & Regional Rules

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
The world geography system creates a living, breathing planet where location matters. Each region has distinct physical properties, ecological systems, cultural identities, and mechanical rules that shape gameplay. The world is not a uniform playground—it's a collection of unique places that demand different strategies, knowledge, and adaptations.

### 1.2 Design Pillars Alignment
- **Knowledge > Gold**: Geographic knowledge (routes, hazards, resources) is invaluable; maps are treasured
- **World Remembers**: Regions change based on player actions; scars of war, prosperity of peace persist
- **Discovery Drives Design**: Players uncover hidden locations, secret passages, regional mysteries
- **Permanent Consequences**: Environmental damage, cultural shifts, territorial changes endure

### 1.3 Scope
This document covers:
- Continental structure and biome distribution
- Regional mechanical modifiers
- Dungeon and special location design
- Climate and seasonal systems
- Natural resource distribution
- Territorial control and borders
- Travel and navigation mechanics

- Special: Tree climbing enabled

**Desert:**
- Water consumption: +100%
- Heat exhaustion risk (midday)
- Cold vulnerability (night)
- Visibility: +20% (open terrain)
- Special: Sandstorm events

**Mountain:**
- Stamina drain: +25% (altitude)
- Fall damage: +50% (steep drops)
- Mining: +30% yield
- Cold resistance required (high peaks)
- Special: Vertical traversal mechanics

**Swamp:**
- Disease chance: +40%
- Movement: -40% (difficult terrain)
- Poison resistance recommended
- Alchemy ingredients: +50% variety
- Special: Sinking/quicksand zones

| Category | Size | Complexity | Threat | Typical Rewards |
|----------|------|------------|--------|-----------------|
| Ruin | Small | Simple | Low-Medium | Common-uncommon gear |
| Tomb | Medium | Moderate | Medium | Rare gear, artifacts |

| Mega-Dungeon | Massive | Multi-level | Very High | Unique artifacts, world-changing |
| Living Dungeon | Variable | Evolving | Extreme | Sentient rewards, curses |

### 4.2 Dungeon Generation

#### 4.2.1 Structural Elements

**Core Components:**
- **Entrance**: Often trapped/guarded, may have puzzle
- **Corridors**: Combat encounters, traps, environmental hazards
- **Chambers**: Mini-bosses, treasure rooms, puzzles
- **Boss Arena**: Final challenge, major reward
- **Exit/Escape**: May differ from entrance, often timed

**Optional Features:**
- Secret rooms (15-25% of dungeons)
- Side quests within dungeon
- NPC prisoners/allies
- Environmental storytelling elements
- Multiple paths/branches

#### 4.2.2 Dungeon Themes

| Theme | Enemies | Hazards | Aesthetic |
|-------|---------|---------|-----------|
| Crypt | Undead | Traps, curses | Stone, bones, darkness |
| Volcano | Fire elementals, dragons | Lava, heat, eruptions | Magma, obsidian, ash |
| Ice Cave | Frost creatures | Cold, ice falls | Crystals, frozen surfaces |
| Jungle Ruin | Beasts, tribal guardians | Vines, poison, collapse | Overgrown stone, nature |
| Sky Temple | Winged beings, elementals | Wind, falling | Floating platforms, clouds |
| Abyss | Demons, horrors | Corruption, madness | Twisted flesh, shadows |
| Machine | Constructs, golems | Electricity, gears | Metal, steam, technology |

### 4.3 Dungeon Mechanics

#### 4.3.1 Dynamic Elements

**Changing Dungeons:**
- Rooms shift/rearrange (periodically or triggered)
- Enemy respawns (prevents clearing exploitation)
- Treasure depletion (finite resources)
- Structural degradation (collapse over time)
- Faction conflicts (enemies fight each other)

**Player Impact:**
- Cleared dungeons stay cleared (temporarily)
- Looted treasure doesn't respawn same items
- Defeated bosses remain dead (permanent)
- Player actions can alter dungeon permanently

#### 4.3.2 Survival Mechanics

**Dungeon Delving:**
- Light sources required (torches, spells)
- Oxygen in sealed/waterlogged areas
- Food/water for extended expeditions
- Rest limitations (dangerous to sleep)
- Escape planning (don't get trapped)

**Risk/Reward:**
- Deeper = more dangerous but better loot
- Timed events (collapsing, rising water)
- Carry limits (can't take everything)
- Recovery cost (healing between fights)

## 5. Climate & Seasons

### 5.1 Seasonal System

#### 5.1.1 Season Effects

| Season | Duration | Temperature | Day Length | Resource Impact |
|--------|----------|-------------|------------|-----------------|
| Spring | 30 days | Mild warming | Increasing | Plants sprout, animals breed |
| Summer | 30 days | Hot | Longest | Peak growth, heat hazards |
| Autumn | 30 days | Cooling | Decreasing | Harvest, preparation |
| Winter | 30 days | Cold/Frigid | Shortest | Scarcity, survival focus |

#### 5.1.2 Seasonal Gameplay Changes

**Spring:**
- +25% foraging success (new growth)
- Animal migrations begin
- Flooding in low areas
- Allergy/respiratory issues (minor)

**Summer:**
- Heat exhaustion risk (desert/plains)
- +50% crop yields
- Water sources may dry
- Wildfire season (random events)

**Autumn:**
- Best hunting (animals fattening)
- Harvest festivals (social events)
- Preparation quests (winter supplies)
- Beautiful foliage (tourism boost)

**Winter:**
- -50% foraging (scarcity)
- Heating requirements (fuel needed)
- Some paths blocked (snow)
- Indoor activities increase
- Ice fishing, winter sports available

### 5.2 Weather System

#### 5.2.1 Weather Types

| Weather | Frequency | Effects | Duration |
|---------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Clear | 40% | No modifiers | 4-12 hours |
| Cloudy | 25% | -10% visibility | 2-8 hours |
| Rain | 15% | -20% visibility, fire difficulty | 1-6 hours |
| Storm | 8% | Lightning strikes, travel dangerous | 1-4 hours |
| Fog | 7% | -60% visibility, stealth +50% | 2-8 hours |
| Snow | 4% (cold regions) | Movement -30%, cold damage | 4-12 hours |
| Blizzard | 1% (cold regions) | Near-zero visibility, lethal cold | 2-8 hours |

#### 5.2.2 Weather Interactions

**Combat:**
- Lightning can strike metal-armored combatants
- Rain extinguishes fires, hampers bows
- Fog enables ambush tactics
- Snow muffles sound (+stealth)

**Travel:**
- Storms delay ships/caravans
- Fog causes navigation errors
- Snow blocks mountain passes
- Clear weather = fastest travel

**Activities:**
- Rain boosts farming (crop growth)
- Clear nights ideal for astronomy
- Storms empower lightning magic
- Fog enables smuggling operations

| Gold | Veins, nuggets | Mountains, rivers | Uncommon |

#### 6.1.2 Node Mechanics

**Depletion:**
- Nodes have finite extracts (10-100 units)
- Over-mining damages node (reduced yield)
- Nodes can "go dormant" (regenerating over months)
- Sustainable harvesting preserves nodes

**Discovery:**
- Visible nodes (surface deposits)
- Hidden nodes (require prospecting skill)
- Buried nodes (need excavation)
- Underwater nodes (diving required)

**Contestation:**
- Valuable nodes attract competitors
- Faction-controlled nodes (access restrictions)
- Monster-guarded nodes (combat required)
- Claim-jumping possible (PvP trigger)

## 7. Territorial Control

### 7.1 Ownership System

#### 7.1.1 Territory Types

| Type | Controller | Access | Rules |
|------|------------|--------|-------|
| Capital City | Major faction | Open (allied) / Restricted (enemy) | Full law enforcement |
| Town/Village | Local lord | Generally open | Local customs apply |
| Wilderness | Unclaimed / Contested | Open | Survival of fittest |
| Dungeon | Monsters / Adventurers | Dangerous | No law |
| Trade Route | Merchant guilds | Toll required | Protected passage |
| Sacred Site | Religious order | Pilgrims welcome | Religious law |

#### 7.1.2 Control Mechanics

**Territory Changes:**
- Conquest through military action
- Purchase/diplomatic transfer
- Rebellion (population uprising)
- Abandonment (depopulation)

**Control Benefits:**
- Tax revenue from territory
- Resource access rights
- Recruitment pools
- Strategic positioning

**Control Responsibilities:**
- Defense against threats
- Infrastructure maintenance
- Population welfare
- Law enforcement

### 7.2 Border Mechanics

#### 7.2.1 Crossing Borders

**Legal Crossings:**
- Guarded checkpoints
- Passport/permit requirements
- Tariffs on goods
- Contraband confiscation

**Illegal Crossings:**
- Stealth required (avoid patrols)
- Smuggling routes (known to locals)
- Natural barriers (rivers, mountains)
- Consequences if caught

#### 7.2.2 Border Conflicts

**Disputed Territories:**
- Multiple factions claim ownership
- Skirmishes common
- Players can influence outcome
- Resolution through war/treaty

**No-Man's Land:**
- Between hostile territories
- Bandit havens
- Refugee camps
- Opportunity for independent settlements

## Appendix A: Regional Examples

### A.1 The Crimson Wastes (High-Threat Desert Region)

**Geography:**
- Vast red-sand desert with scattered oases
- Ancient buried cities half-submerged in dunes
- Extreme temperature swings (blazing days, freezing nights)

**Regional Rules:**
- Water consumption +200%
- Fire spells +50% power
- Water spells -50% power, double cost
- Sandstorm events (weekly, severe visibility loss)

**Resources:**
- Sunstone (exclusive gem)
- Desert glass (crafting material)
- Ancient artifacts (ruin diving)
- Scorpion venom (alchemy)

**Inhabitants:**
- Nomadic tribes (neutral if respected)
- Sand dragons (apex predators)
- Genie binding cults (mysterious faction)
- Undead pharaohs (dungeon bosses)

**Player Strategies:**
- Travel at night, rest during day
- Stockpile water before entering
- Hire local guides (worth the cost)
- Avoid sandstorm season if possible

### A.2 The Verdant Maze (Living Jungle Region)

**Geography:**
- Dense rainforest with canopy layers
- Rivers that change course seasonally
- Trees that slowly migrate (over weeks)

**Regional Rules:**
- Pathfinding skill essential (landscape shifts)
- Plants react to player presence (paths open/close)
- Rapid regrowth (cleared paths close in days)
- Poison resistance highly valuable

**Resources:**
- Heartwood (unbreakable crafting material)
- Exotic poisons and antidotes
- Rare jungle fruits (powerful buffs)
- Dinosaur hides (legendary armor)

**Inhabitants:**
- Tribal societies (complex reputation system)
- Giant insects (hostile unless befriended)
- Druid circles (neutral, powerful)
- Lost civilizations (dungeon complexes)

**Unique Mechanics:**
- Canopy traversal (rope bridges, tree paths)
- River boat navigation (changing routes)
- Tribal diplomacy (gift economy)
- Ancient ruin puzzles (environmental manipulation)

# Skills & Progression System

## 1. Core Philosophy

### Growth Through Action, Not Levels
Progression is not measured by arbitrary XP bars or level gates. Instead, characters grow through **doing**, **learning**, and **being remembered**. The world rewards curiosity, mastery, and consequence rather than grind.

**Design Mantra:**
> "You don't become stronger because you killed 100 wolves. You become stronger because the wolves taught you how to survive."

### Key Principles
- **No Traditional Levels:** Character power grows organically through skill usage and reputation.
- **Action-Based Mastery:** Using a sword makes you better with swords; reading books improves knowledge skills.
- **Invisible Counters:** Many progression triggers are hidden, encouraging experimentation.
- **Three-Tier Skill System:** Common (learnable), Legendary (earned), Forbidden (costly).
- **Role Synergy:** Skills complement role perspectives without restricting freedom.

## 2. Progression Layers

### Layer 1: Character Foundation
Basic attributes that improve through general activity:
| Attribute | How It Grows | Effect |
|-----------|--------------|--------|
| **Vitality** | Surviving damage, enduring harsh environments | Increased health, resistance to status effects |
| **Stamina** | Running, climbing, fighting for extended periods | Larger stamina pool, faster regeneration |
| **Awareness** | Discovering secrets, noticing clues, tracking | Enhanced perception radius, hidden object detection |
| **Resilience** | Enduring extreme weather, poison, disease | Better environmental resistance, faster recovery |

**Note:** These do not have numeric levels. They manifest as tangible improvements (e.g., less stamina drain, longer breath underwater).

### Layer 2: Skill Proficiency
Skills improve through repeated use and mastery challenges:
- **Usage-Based Growth:** Swing a sword → sword techniques unlock naturally.
- **Mastery Thresholds:** After enough use, hidden "breakthrough" moments occur (new combo, reduced cost).
- **Diminishing Returns:** Mindless repetition yields less growth; varied, challenging use accelerates it.

### Layer 3: Reputation & Recognition
The world acknowledges your deeds:
- **Faction Standing:** Groups trust/respect/fear you based on actions.
- **Titles & Epithets:** Earned through milestones (e.g., "Dragonfriend," "Shadowwalker").
- **Legacy Marks:** Permanent narrative tags affecting NPC interactions (see NPC System).

## 3. Three-Tier Skill Structure

### Tier 1: Common Skills
Available to all players through practice and training.

#### Categories:
| Category | Examples | Acquisition |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Combat** | Swordplay, Archery, Blocking, Dodging | Use weapons, train with NPCs |
| **Survival** | Swimming, Climbing, Stealth, Foraging | Explore dangerous areas |
| **Crafting** | Blacksmithing, Alchemy, Cooking, Enchanting | Craft items, learn from masters |
| **Knowledge** | Ancient Languages, Lore Reading, Map Making | Read books, translate texts |
| **Social** | Persuasion, Bartering, Leadership, Deception | Interact with NPCs/players |

**Progression Mechanism:**
- **Novice:** Basic functionality (e.g., swing sword).
- **Adept:** Unlocks advanced techniques (e.g., parry-counter combo).
- **Master:** Unique flair/style emerges (e.g., signature move).
- **Grandmaster:** Teaches others, creates new techniques (player-driven innovation).

### Tier 2: Legendary Skills
Unlocked through unique achievements, not grinding. These define heroes.

#### Examples:
| Skill | Unlock Condition | Effect |
|-------|------------------|--------|
| **Dragon Slayer** | Defeat an Ancient Dragon solo | Damage bonus vs. dragons, fear immunity |
| **Master Explorer** | Discover 50+ hidden locations without maps | Reveals secret paths on minimap |
| **Beast Whisperer** | Befriend 10+ rare creatures without combat | Animals approach peacefully, provide aid |
| **Storm Caller** | Survive 100 storms while casting magic | Cast spells during storms with doubled power |
| **Shadow Walker** | Complete 20 missions undetected | Become invisible in shadows for short bursts |
| **Lorekeeper** | Translate all fragments of a lost language | Access forbidden libraries, decode ancient traps |

**Characteristics:**
- **Unique per Player:** Only those who meet conditions obtain them.
- **World-Recognized:** NPCs comment on these skills ("You're the one who tamed the wolf king!").
- **Non-Transferable:** Cannot be traded or taught directly (but can inspire others).

### Tier 3: Forbidden Skills
Powerful abilities with permanent consequences. Obtained through dark/risky paths.

#### Examples:
| Skill | Unlock Condition | Cost/Consequence |
|-------|------------------|------------------|
| **Blood Magic** | Sacrifice own health or allies to cast spells | Permanent max HP reduction, NPCs fear you |
| **Necromancy** | Resurrect dead creatures using forbidden rituals | Mark of the Desecrator, hunted by holy orders |
| **Vampire Curse** | Accept gift from ancient vampire entity | Weak to sunlight, must feed on blood regularly |
| **Soul Binding** | Trap souls in weapons for power | Soul fragments haunt player, sanity mechanics |
| **Reality Tear** | Manipulate space-time using unstable artifacts | Random teleportation glitches, attracts cosmic horrors |

**Design Intent:** Forbidden skills are not "evil choices" but **high-risk/high-reward** paths that fundamentally alter gameplay and world reaction.

## 4. Mastery & Breakthrough System

### 4.1 Invisible Progress Bars
Players never see "87/100 Sword XP." Instead:
- Subtle hints appear ("Your swings feel more fluid").
- NPCs notice ("You've improved since last we met").
- New options unlock in dialogue/combat/crafting.

### 4.2 Breakthrough Moments
After sufficient practice + specific conditions, players experience **epiphanies**:
- **Trigger:** Varies (e.g., defeat strong enemy, meditate at shrine, read specific book).
- **Effect:** Unlocks advanced technique or passive bonus.
- **Example:** 
  - *Condition:* Parry 50 attacks + read "Art of Defense" book.
  - *Breakthrough:* Unlock "Perfect Parry" (stuns attacker).

### 4.3 Skill Decay (Optional Hardcore Mode)
Skills unused for long periods may dull:
- **Soft Decay:** Slight efficiency loss (reversible with practice).
- **Hard Decay:** Lose advanced techniques until re-trained.
- **Purpose:** Encourages diverse playstyles, prevents "set-and-forget" builds.

## 5. Role-Specific Progression

While skills are open to all, roles accelerate certain paths:

| Role | Accelerated Skills | Unique Starting Bonuses |
|------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| **Wanderer** | Survival, Exploration, Stealth | Starts with partial map of region |
| **Treasure Hunter** | Lockpicking, Appraising, Tracking | Compass points to nearby valuables |
| **Wizard/Scholar** | Magic, Languages, Lore Reading | Can decipher 1 ancient text immediately |
| **Herbalist** | Alchemy, Botany, Poison Resistance | Identifies all plants in starting zone |
| **Blacksmith** | Smithing, Mining, Repair | Crafts higher-tier items from day 1 |
| **Nightborn** | Night Combat, Shadow Stealth, Dark Vision | Stronger at night, weaker by day |

**Note:** Roles do not **restrict** skills—they **shape** how easily they're acquired and perceived.

## 6. Knowledge as Progression

Learning is power. Knowledge skills unlock new capabilities:

### 6.1 Language Mastery
- **Fragmented Learning:** Find dictionary pages, hear native speakers, translate inscriptions.
- **Progressive Fluency:** 
  - *Level 1:* Recognize symbols.
  - *Level 2:* Understand basic phrases.
  - *Level 3:* Full conversation, access to secret dialogues.
- **Application:** Unlock hidden dungeons, negotiate with ancient beings, read cursed scrolls safely.

### 6.2 Lore Accumulation
- **Book Collections:** Completing sets reveals hidden maps/quests (see Knowledge Economy).
- **Rumor Verification:** Confirming rumors grants "Truthseeker" bonuses (better info quality).
- **Historical Insight:** Knowing past events predicts future world changes.

### 6.3 Map Making
- **Exploration Rewards:** Mapping uncharted areas grants cartography skill increases.
- **Shared Knowledge:** Sell maps to other players (economic progression).
- **Annotation System:** Add notes/dangers/treasures to personal maps.

## 7. Profession Mastery

Crafting and gathering professions have independent progression:

### 7.1 Skill Tiers
| Tier | Requirement | Unlocks |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Apprentice** | First successful craft | Basic recipes |
| **Journeyman** | 50+ items crafted | Advanced techniques, tool upgrades |
| **Artisan** | Create masterpiece item | Custom designs, apprentices (NPCs/players) |
| **Master** | Recognized by guild/kingdom | Exclusive materials, legendary recipes |
| **Legend** | World-first achievement | Named items, historical record |

### 7.2 Quality Factors
Item quality depends on:
- **Player Skill:** Higher proficiency = better base quality.
- **Tool Condition:** Worn tools reduce success chance.
- **Environment:** Crafting in rain affects metalwork; moon phase affects enchantments.
- **Resource Rarity:** Rare materials enable higher-tier results.
- **Technique Discovery:** Experimenting unlocks hidden methods (fusion system).

### 7.3 Signature Creations
Masters can create **named items** bearing their creator's name:
- *"Rowan's Eternal Blade"* (crafted by Blacksmith Rowan).
- Item history tracks owner lineage (see Object Memory).

## 8. Failure as Progression

Failure teaches lessons:
- **Combat Loss:** Learn enemy patterns, unlock "Analyze Weakness" skill.
- **Crafting Disaster:** Discover why recipe failed, gain insight for next attempt.
- **Social Rejection:** Understand faction values, adjust approach.
- **Exploration Dead-End:** Mark area as dangerous, share warning with community.

**No Permanent Lockouts:** Failure redirects progress; it doesn't end it.

## 10. Anti-Grind Measures

### 10.1 Diminishing Returns
Mindless repetition yields less growth:
- Killing same weak enemy repeatedly → reduced skill gain after threshold.
- **Solution:** Seek varied challenges for optimal growth.

### 10.2 Fatigue System (Optional)
Extended play sessions reduce efficiency:
- **Purpose:** Discourage botting/marathon grinding.
- **Mechanic:** Stamina regen slows, skill gain reduced after X hours.

### 10.3 Challenge Scaling
Skills grow fastest when facing **appropriate challenges**:
- Novice fighting bosses → minimal growth (overwhelmed).
- Master fighting novices → minimal growth (no learning).
- **Sweet Spot:** Fighting slightly stronger opponents → maximum growth.

## 11. Success Metrics

Track to validate progression design:
- **Skill Diversity:** Average number of skills used per player.
- **Breakthrough Frequency:** How often players experience epiphany moments.
- **Legendary Skill Distribution:** % of players earning each legendary skill.
- **Forbidden Skill Adoption:** Risk/reward balance assessment.
- **Retention Correlation:** Do players with diverse skills stay longer?

---

## 12. Technical Considerations

### 12.1 Data Tracking
- Store usage counters for all skills (server-side).
- Track breakthrough conditions invisibly.
- Maintain skill history for legacy records.

### 12.2 Balance Challenges
- Prevent power creep from stacking too many legendary skills.
- Ensure forbidden skills have meaningful consequences.
- Balance role acceleration without making non-role skills useless.

### 12.3 Exploit Prevention
- Detect abnormal skill gain patterns (botting).
- Validate breakthrough conditions server-side.
- Limit skill decay exploitation (logging in/out cycles).

## 13. Future Expansion

- **Skill Fusion:** Combine two mastered skills to create hybrid abilities.
- **Teaching System:** Masters train apprentices (player-to-player skill transfer).
- **Dynamic Skill Trees:** Skills evolve based on playstyle (branching paths).
- **Seasonal Mastery:** Temporary skills tied to events/festivals.

---

## 14. Design Checklist

Before implementing any skill:
- [ ] Does it encourage active play over passive grinding?
- [ ] Are there multiple paths to mastery?
- [ ] Does it integrate with knowledge/economy/NPC systems?
- [ ] Is there a clear "breakthrough" moment?
- [ ] For forbidden skills: Are consequences permanent and meaningful?
- [ ] Does it support role identity without restricting freedom?

---

## 15. Summary

The Skills & Progression system replaces traditional leveling with **organic growth through action, discovery, and consequence**. By hiding counters, rewarding diverse play, and tying advancement to world recognition, it ensures every player's journey feels unique and earned.

> "Progress isn't a number. It's the story of what you've done, what you've learned, and how the world remembers you."

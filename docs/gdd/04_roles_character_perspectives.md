# Roles & Character Perspectives

## Overview
Roles in this game are not traditional classes with fixed abilities. Instead, they are **perspectives** that fundamentally change how players perceive and interact with the world. Each role acts as a perceptual filter, revealing different aspects of the same environment.

## Core Philosophy
- **Perspective Over Power**: Roles define what you notice, not just what you can do
- **Information Asymmetry**: Different roles see different things in the same location
- **Flexible Progression**: Players can develop multiple roles over time
- **Unique Experiences**: The same world tells different stories to different roles

## Starting Roles

### Wanderer
**Philosophy**: The pure explorer, unburdened by specialization
**Starting Conditions**:
- Begins with no world map
- Map reveals only explored areas (fog of war)
- No starting bonuses or penalties

**Strengths**:
- Enhanced exploration speed
- Better stamina regeneration while traveling
- Increased chance to discover hidden paths
- Natural navigation abilities

**Weaknesses**:
- No specialized combat abilities
- Limited crafting options initially
- Must learn everything through experience

**Unique Perceptions**:
- Notices subtle environmental clues others miss
- Senses nearby points of interest
- Detects weather pattern changes earlier

---

### Treasure Hunter
**Philosophy**: Seeker of hidden wealth and ancient artifacts
**Starting Conditions**:
- Begins with a basic compass
- Access to treasure-hunting tools

**Strengths**:
- Enhanced ability to find hidden loot
- Better at detecting traps and secret compartments
- Increased carry capacity for valuables
- Can appraise item values accurately

**Weaknesses**:
- Weaker in direct combat
- Slower movement when heavily laden
- Attracts thieves and bandits

**Unique Perceptions**:
- Sees glimmers from buried treasures
- Notices pressure plates and hidden mechanisms
- Detects magical auras on valuable items
- Reads subtle signs of previous visitors

---

### Wizard / Scholar
**Philosophy**: Master of arcane knowledge and magical forces
**Starting Conditions**:
- Begins with a spellbook containing basic spells
- Can read some ancient languages

**Strengths**:
- Powerful magical abilities
- Can decipher magical texts and ancient languages
- Identifies magical properties of items
- Casts utility spells for exploration

**Weaknesses**:
- Weak physical combat capabilities
- Low health and stamina pools
- Requires mana management
- Vulnerable to magic resistance

**Unique Perceptions**:
- Senses magical energy concentrations
- Sees invisible magical barriers
- Detects enchantments and curses
- Reads residual magical signatures
- Identifies ley lines and power nodes

---

### Herbalist
**Philosophy**: Guardian of natural remedies and botanical knowledge
**Starting Conditions**:
- Begins with herb pouch and basic identification guide
- Knowledge of common plant properties

**Strengths**:
- Identifies all plants instantly
- Crafts powerful potions and remedies
- Resistant to poisons and diseases
- Can cultivate rare plants

**Weaknesses**:
- Weak melee combat abilities
- Fragile armor options
- Requires time to gather and prepare materials

**Unique Perceptions**:
- Sees plant locations highlighted
- Identifies poisonous vs. beneficial plants
- Detects soil quality and growing conditions
- Notices seasonal plant cycles
- Senses ecological imbalances

---

### Blacksmith
**Philosophy**: Master craftsman of weapons and armor
**Starting Conditions**:
- Begins with basic tool kit
- Knowledge of common metals and materials

**Strengths**:
- Crafts and repairs equipment efficiently
- Better mining and metalworking yields
- Can modify and upgrade gear
- Identifies material qualities instantly

**Weaknesses**:
- Slower movement speed
- Heavier equipment reduces agility
- Limited magical abilities
- Requires access to forge facilities

**Unique Perceptions**:
- Sees ore deposits through rock
- Identifies metal purity and quality
- Detects structural weaknesses in armor/weapons
- Notices heat sources and volcanic activity
- Reads craftsmanship signatures on items

---

### Nightborn
**Philosophy**: Creature of darkness, empowered by shadow
**Starting Conditions**:
- Begins with enhanced night vision
- Natural affinity for shadow magic

**Strengths**:
- Significantly stronger at night
- Enhanced night vision
- Stealth abilities in darkness
- Resistant to shadow-based attacks

**Weaknesses**:
- Weaker during daylight hours
- Sunlight causes discomfort or damage
- Limited visibility in bright conditions
- Some NPCs distrust Nightborn

**Unique Perceptions**:
- Sees clearly in complete darkness
- Detects hidden creatures in shadows
- Notices lunar cycle effects
- Senses supernatural presences at night
- Reads shadow patterns and movements

## Role Mechanics

### Perception Filters
Each role applies a unique filter to the game world:

**Visual Filters**:
- Highlighted objects specific to role
- Color-coded environmental cues
- Role-specific UI overlays
- Unique minimap markers

**Audio Filters**:
- Role-specific sound cues
- Enhanced hearing for relevant sounds
- Musical themes that change based on role

**Dialogue Filters**:
- Different NPC dialogue options
- Role-specific conversation topics
- Unique information revealed in conversations
- Varied quest availability

### Role Progression
Players develop their chosen role through:

1. **Usage-Based Growth**: Performing role-specific actions increases proficiency
2. **Knowledge Acquisition**: Learning new techniques and secrets
3. **Equipment Mastery**: Obtaining role-specific gear
4. **Reputation Building**: Becoming known for role expertise
5. **Mentor Relationships**: Finding masters to learn from

### Multi-Role Development
Players are not locked into a single role:
- Can develop multiple roles simultaneously
- Some roles complement each other (Herbalist + Alchemist)
- Some roles conflict (Wizard + Necromancer may cause faction issues)
- Switching focus between roles is fluid but requires investment

## Information Asymmetry Examples

### Same Location, Different Experiences

**Location**: Ancient Ruin Entrance

**Wanderer Sees**:
- Weathered stone archway
- Faint footprints leading inside
- Wind patterns suggesting hollow spaces beyond

**Treasure Hunter Sees**:
- Glint of gold embedded in stonework
- Pressure plate near entrance
- Signs of previous looting attempts

**Wizard Sees**:
- Residual magical barrier (faded)
- Arcane symbols above doorway
- Ley line convergence point

**Herbalist Sees**:
- Rare moss growing on north side
- Poisonous vines blocking side passage
- Medicinal herbs in courtyard beyond

**Blacksmith Sees**:
- Quality of stonework indicates master craftsmen
- Metal reinforcements visible beneath stone
- Structural weakness in eastern wall

**Nightborn Sees**:
- Shadow creatures lurking in corners
- Moon-aligned symbols glowing faintly
- Dark energy emanating from depths

### Collaborative Discovery
When players of different roles group together:
- They share unique perceptions
- Combine clues to solve complex puzzles
- Access areas requiring multiple role abilities
- Create comprehensive understanding of locations

## Role Selection & Changes

### Initial Selection
- Players choose starting role during character creation
- Choice affects early game experience significantly
- Can experiment with other roles later

### Role Evolution
- Roles can evolve based on player actions
- Hidden roles unlock through specific achievements
- Forbidden roles require accepting permanent consequences
- Legendary roles earned through extraordinary feats

### Respec Options
- Players can shift role focus gradually
- No hard reset of progress
- Previous role knowledge remains useful
- Transition periods allow adaptation

## Balance Considerations

### Ensuring Viability
- All roles have unique value in group settings
- Solo play viable for all roles with different approaches
- No role strictly superior in all situations
- Context-dependent advantages (time, location, objectives)

### Preventing Exploitation
- Role-switching has opportunity costs
- Cannot exploit multiple role perceptions simultaneously without penalty
- Server validates role-specific discoveries
- Anti-cheat measures for impossible perceptions

## Community Impact

### Role Diversity Benefits
- Encourages group composition variety
- Creates natural interdependence
- Fosters knowledge trading between roles
- Generates diverse content creation (different role perspectives)

### Social Dynamics
- Players seek specialists for specific tasks
- Role-based guilds and communities form
- Mentors teach newcomers their role
- Cross-role friendships develop through cooperation

## Success Metrics

The role system succeeds when:
- Players genuinely experience the world differently
- Groups naturally form with complementary roles
- Community discussions highlight role-specific discoveries
- Content creators showcase same location from multiple perspectives
- Players feel their role choice meaningfully impacts their journey
- No role feels obsolete or strictly inferior

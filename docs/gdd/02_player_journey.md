# 02. Player Journey

## Overview

This document traces the player's experience from character creation through long-term play, highlighting key milestones, emotional beats, and system introductions.

---

## Phase 1: Character Creation (First 5 Minutes)

### Decisions
1. **Character Name** - Visible to other players; becomes part of world history
2. **Appearance** - Customizable features; no stat implications
3. **Starting Role** - Defines initial perspective and abilities (cannot be changed easily)
4. **Optional Backstory** - 2-3 sentences for roleplay; may influence minor NPC reactions

### Available Starting Roles
- **Wanderer** - Balanced explorer; starts with partial map of starting region
- **Treasure Hunter** - Enhanced clue detection; starts with compass
- **Wizard/Scholar** - Magical perception; can sense arcane residues
- **Herbalist** - Plant identification; starts with basic potion recipes
- **Blacksmith** - Ore detection; enhanced crafting/repair abilities
- **Nightborn** - Night vision; stronger at night, weaker during day

### What Players Don't Know Yet
- The full impact of role choice on information asymmetry
- That roles can be expanded but not easily changed
- That their name will be remembered by the world
- That backstory choices might echo later

---

## Phase 2: The Forgotten Valley (Hours 1-3)

### Spawn Experience
Players begin in **The Forgotten Valley**, but at different locations:
- Fishing village by the lake
- Abandoned watchtower on the hill
- Forest camp among ancient trees
- Ruined chapel overgrown with vines
- Mountain pass overlooking the valley

### First Discoveries
**Minute 5-15:**
- Explore immediate surroundings
- Notice empty map filling as they explore
- Meet first NPC who remembers their name after one conversation

**Minute 15-45:**
- Find first hidden element (secret path, rare plant, hidden compartment)
- Experience first role-specific discovery (Wizard sees magic, Herbalist identifies plants)
- Receive first rumor from NPC (may be true or false)

**Minute 45-90:**
- Reach first settlement
- Learn about knowledge economy (players trading maps, info)
- Update personal journal with first entries
- Possibly trigger first small Legacy Scenario indicator (subtle, unexplained event)

**Minute 90-180:**
- First major discovery (dungeon entrance, ancient ruin, hidden cave)
- First combat encounter (optional; multiple solutions possible)
- First rest point experience (inn, campfire, temple)
- End first session with "I need to come back and explore more" feeling

### Emotional Goals
- Curiosity about the wider world
- Surprise that the world responds to them
- Intrigue about mysteries not yet solved
- Comfort with self-directed play

---

## Phase 3: Finding Their Path (Hours 3-10)

### System Introductions

#### Knowledge Economy
- First knowledge trade (buying a map, selling discovery coordinates)
- Realization that information has value
- Learning to use/trade rumors

#### Relationship Building
- NPCs begin recognizing repeat visits
- First gift exchange with NPC
- Unlocking new dialogue through relationship progression

#### Personal Journal
- Journal auto-fills with discoveries
- Sketches of creatures/plants appear
- Rumors logged with verification status
- Maps saved with personal notes

#### Rest Points
- First time using inn/campfire to save
- Experience time shift (day/night cycle)
- Hear gossip about world events
- Receive letters or messages from NPCs

### First Major Choice
Player encounters situation with multiple solutions:
- Help a traveling merchant (reward: reputation, future discount)
- Ignore them (no consequence immediately, but remembered)
- Rob them (short-term gain, long-term consequences weeks later)

### Milestone: First Legacy Indicator
Subtle hint that the world is watching:
- Animal behaves unusually around them
- NPC mentions hearing about their deeds
- Strange dream at rest point
- Mysterious item appears in inventory

---

## Phase 4: Building Identity (Hours 10-30)

### Role Mastery
- Deepening understanding of role abilities
- First role-specific scenario unlocked
- Meeting NPCs who react specially to their role
- Discovering areas only their role can access

### Relationship Networks
- 5+ NPCs with established relationships
- First NPC seeks them out unprompted
- Village-wide reputation developing
- First regional Mark earned (Protector, Scholar, etc.)

### Knowledge Accumulation
- First ancient language partially deciphered
- Map of starting region mostly complete
- Multiple books/texts collected
- First book combination puzzle solved

### Equipment with History
- Primary weapon/tool gaining XP
- First named item acquired (with history tab)
- Equipment begins reflecting playstyle
- First fusion experiment attempted

### First World Event
Witness or participate in server-wide event:
- Celestial phenomenon (eclipse, meteor shower)
- Seasonal festival
- Boss defeat unlocking new area
- Natural disaster changing geography

---

## Phase 5: Legacy Scenarios (Hours 30-100)

### First Major Legacy Scenario
**Trigger:** Invisible conditions met over weeks of play
**Event:** Unexpected encounter (animal leads somewhere, NPC appears with offer, location changes)
**Choice:** Multiple paths with different outcomes
**Reward:** Not just power—unique access, knowledge, relationship, or title
**Aftermath:** World permanently changed for this player

### Community Recognition
- Other players recognize their name/reputation
- Invited to group expeditions
- Asked for information/guidance
- Possibly featured in community content

### Multi-Regional Presence
- Known differently in various kingdoms
- Reputation varies by faction
- Some regions welcome them; others wary
- Travel becomes meaningful (different experiences per location)

### Deeper Mysteries
- First Ancient Entity encountered (not necessarily fought)
- Unexplainable geography discovered
- Contradictory lore pieces collected
- Personal theory developed about world history

---

## Phase 6: Long-Term Play (100+ Hours)

### Legacy Established
- Name recorded in libraries/monuments
- Statue or memorial possibly built
- NPC children remember stories about them
- Multiple Marks defining their identity

### World Changes Witnessed
- Villages prospered or declined based on actions
- Trade routes opened/closed
- Bridges rebuilt, cities unlocked
- Geography permanently altered

### Mentorship Role
- Helping new players learn the world
- Sharing maps/knowledge freely
- Leading community investigations
- Creating content (guides, theories, stories)

### Continuing Discovery
- Still finding new areas after 100+ hours
- Unresolved Legacy Scenarios in progress
- Ancient Entities yet to encounter
- Personal goals still being pursued

### Server-Wide Impact
- Participated in major world events
- Collective achievements unlocked
- Part of community history
- Future players will encounter their legacy

## Key Decision Points

### Early Game (Hours 1-10)
| Decision | Impact |
|----------|--------|
| Role selection | Determines initial perspective and access |
| First NPC relationships | Sets reputation trajectory |
| Knowledge hoarding vs. sharing | Affects community standing |
| Combat approach (aggressive/peaceful) | Influences invisible counters |

### Mid Game (Hours 10-50)
| Decision | Impact |
|----------|--------|
| Which regions to explore | Determines available scenarios |
| Faction alignments | Opens/closes content paths |
| Legacy Scenario choices | Creates unique story branches |
| Resource investment (crafting/combat/exploration) | Shapes character identity |

### Late Game (Hours 50+)
| Decision | Impact |
|----------|--------|
| How to use accumulated influence | Can help communities or individuals |
| Whether to mentor newcomers | Affects community health |
| Final Legacy Scenario resolutions | Determines ending variations |
| What legacy to leave behind | Becomes part of world history |

## Pacing Guidelines

### Content Release Rate
- **Hours 1-5:** Linear introduction to systems
- **Hours 5-20:** Branching paths become available
- **Hours 20-50:** Multiple concurrent storylines
- **Hours 50+:** Player-driven narrative dominates

### Difficulty Curve
- No level gates; difficulty is self-selected
- Clear telegraphing of dangerous areas
- Multiple solutions always available
- Retreat option never removed

### Novelty Maintenance
- New discoveries every session (small or large)
- Unresolved mysteries always present
- World changes provide fresh context
- Community theories generate new curiosity

---

## Failure States (And Recovery)

### Soft Failures
**Situation:** Player makes poor choice (angers NPC, enters area unprepared)
**Consequence:** Temporary setback, not game over
**Recovery:** Alternative paths always exist; time heals some wounds

### Permanent Consequences
**Situation:** Irreversible action (NPC death, destroyed bridge)
**Consequence:** World adapts; successor emerges; new path opens
**Recovery:** Different experience, not worse experience

### Getting Stuck
**Situation:** Player can't progress on chosen path
**Solution:** 
- Journal provides subtle hints
- Community resources available
- Alternative objectives always exist
- Staff intervention only as last resort

---

## Onboarding Philosophy

### What We Teach Explicitly
- Basic movement and interaction
- How to rest/save
- Journal usage
- Trading interface
- Combat basics (if engaged)

### What We Let Players Discover
- Role abilities through use
- Legacy Scenario triggers
- Book combination puzzles
- Hidden area requirements
- NPC relationship mechanics

### Community's Role in Onboarding
- Veteran players naturally mentor newcomers
- Wikis and guides fill knowledge gaps
- Discord/Reddit answer questions
- In-game tools support knowledge sharing

---

## Metrics for Journey Health

### Completion Indicators
- % of players reaching each phase
- Average time spent in each phase
- Drop-off points requiring investigation

### Engagement Indicators
- Sessions per week by phase
- Variety of activities per session
- Social interactions frequency

### Satisfaction Indicators
- Self-reported enjoyment surveys
- "Surprised by world" frequency
- Legacy Scenario completion satisfaction

---

*The player journey should feel like writing a unique story, not completing a checklist. Any phase that feels mandatory or linear must be reworked.*

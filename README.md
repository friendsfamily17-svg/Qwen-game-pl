# Living World RPG - Game Design Repository

> **A persistent living world where discovery, relationships, and player-created stories matter more than becoming stronger.**

---

## 🎯 Project Vision

This is not an MMORPG. This is a **World Simulation RPG** where:
- Knowledge is more valuable than gold
- The world remembers every action
- Legends are stories, not boss fights
- Players are travelers, not chosen heroes
- Wonder drives every design decision

## 🎮 The Nine Design Pillars

1. **Wonder** - "What's beyond that mountain?"
2. **Living World** - The world changes, remembers, evolves
3. **Knowledge** - Information is the most valuable resource
4. **Legacy Scenarios** - Hidden events triggered by player actions
5. **Roles** - Different perspectives, not rigid classes
6. **Exploration** - True discovery of the unexplainable
7. **Community** - Collective detective work and shared history
8. **Personal Story** - Unique journeys shaped by invisible counters
9. **Consequences** - Actions ripple through time permanently

## ⚠️ Critical Design Constraints

1. **No Quest Markers** - Discovery must be organic
2. **No Traditional Levels** - Growth through action, not XP
3. **No Respawnable Legends** - Ancient Entities are unique
4. **No Invisible Walls** - Geography follows logical rules
5. **No Binary Morality** - Consequences are nuanced, not "good/evil"
6. **No Hand-Holding** - Players learn through experimentation
7. **No Disposable Content** - Everything has potential meaning

## 📞 Quick Reference

### Core Philosophy in One Sentence
> "A living multiplayer world where discovery, relationships, and the stories created by players matter more than simply becoming stronger."

### Elevator Pitch
Imagine entering a world where nobody knows everything—not the players, not the NPCs, not even history itself. You're not the chosen hero; you're a traveler uncovering ancient mysteries, building relationships, and leaving a legacy. Knowledge is more valuable than gold, the world remembers your actions, and every discovery feels earned because it emerges from interacting with living systems rather than following quest markers.

### What Makes This Unique?
- **Knowledge Economy:** Information is traded, sold, and hoarded like physical goods
- **Legacy Scenarios:** Hidden events trigger based on invisible counters and player behavior
- **Information Asymmetry:** Different roles see different aspects of the same world
- **Permanent Consequences:** NPC deaths, world changes, and generational memory
- **Wonder-Driven Design:** Every system prioritizes curiosity and mystery
